# Marine glyphs

White-on-transparent 512px masters, tinted at draw time through
`BlendMode.srcIn` — see `lib/features/map/marine_glyphs.dart` for why they are
images rather than Material icons or drawn paths.

## How these were made

Generated by ChatGPT (image generation, not SVG) against a written brief asking
for the same flat illustration style as the app icon: solid filled shapes, no
outlines, gradients or shadows, confident geometric silhouettes of real boats,
laid out as one 3x2 sheet, pure white on a fully transparent background.

The first attempt at this set asked for **SVG path data** instead. That was the
wrong ask — hand-written path coordinates are one of the weakest things a
language model produces, and the result was thin, mean line art that looked
nothing like the app icon. Ask it to draw, not to write geometry.

The sheet was then sliced per cell, trimmed to each icon's alpha bounding box,
padded to a square with ~7% margin and scaled to 512px, so every glyph has the
same optical weight regardless of its natural aspect.
