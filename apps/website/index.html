Passage desk

One plan, from desk to deck.

  • Editable rhumb-line legs with distance, course and estimated time
  • Harbours, marinas, anchorages, moorings and seamark context
  • Time-aware wind, model currents, tides and daylight planning
  • GPX export and verified links into the Tide and Seek app

Tide and Seek is a planning and coordination aid, not a nautical chart, certified navigation system or substitute for current official information and seamanship.
