Regional depth shading and contours: EMODnet Bathymetry DTM 2024, EU, CC BY 4.0. Worldwide fallback and global overview: GEBCO 2026 Grid. Shallow contours are derived on demand for the visible map, with detail increasing as you zoom in; GEBCO is substantially coarser, and neither source describes dredged marina basins or safe under-keel clearance. US chart imagery: NOAA Chart Display Service. Wind, sea level and ocean-current guidance: Open-Meteo model forecasts, CC BY 4.0. Current and sea-level fields are about 8 km resolution and explicitly unsuitable for coastal navigation. Solent LAT/MSL datum transforms: TICON-4 via Neaps, CC BY 4.0. None of these model values is observed pilotage data.
