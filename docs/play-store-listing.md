## App name

Tide and Seek

## Short description

Offline-first sailing companion for passages, tides, wind, AIS and MOB.

## Full description

Tide and Seek is a private field-test sailing companion for solo sailors and
small crews.

Plan and record a local voyage without creating an account. Keep a passage,
track, course, speed, bearing, distance and voyage recap on the phone, with GPX
import and export and an optional offline voice pack.

The underway map is flat, starts farther out and stays under your control while
you pan, pinch, zoom and reposition it. Optional crew sharing uses a short code
or QR invitation and can exchange temporary voyage state directly with nearby
devices.

Current test features include:

- offline astronomical tide-height predictions for three Solent stations,
  clearly labelled as calculated and non-official;
- time-selectable Open-Meteo wind, gust, weather and wave forecasts with source,
  valid time and freshness;
- received AIS targets from a configured boat-local NMEA TCP source, plus a
  clearly labelled synthetic replay for testing;
- a manual MOB button that fixes the available position on the map and shows
  elapsed time, distance and bearing until recovery or false alarm is recorded;
- passage marks, route legs, navigation instruments, recorded tracks, voyage
  history and simulation; and
- offline-capable OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap context with visible attribution.

Important limitations

This test build does not contain licensed nautical charts, soundings, depth
contours, tidal streams, network AIS, collision alarms or automatic distress
transmission. AIS never shows every vessel. Forecasts and calculated tides are
not observations. The MOB tool does not contact emergency services.

Tide and Seek is not a certified chartplotter or navigation system and is not a
substitute for official charts, Notices to Mariners, proper passage planning,
sound seamanship or appropriate emergency equipment. Nothing in this field-test
build should be relied on for safe navigation at sea.

## Proposed Play declarations

- App access: no restricted sign-in; simulation exposes the group workflow to
  reviewers without credentials or another device.
- Ads: none.
- Target audience: 18 and over only.
- Category: Maps & navigation.
- Government app: no.
- Financial features: none.
- Health: Emergency and First Aid. The app provides a non-medical MOB recovery
  aid and can hold sailor-entered emergency/medical notes for deliberate crew
  sharing; it does not diagnose, treat, or claim to be a medical device.
- Data safety: approximate location, precise location, diagnostics, and app
  interactions. Each is collected and shared, optional, not treated as
  ephemeral, and encrypted in transit. Location is used for app functionality;
  diagnostics and app interactions are used for analytics. There is no account
  creation or publisher-operated deletion-request service.
